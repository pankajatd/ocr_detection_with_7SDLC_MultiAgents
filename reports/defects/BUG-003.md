## 1. Defect Description
Skew angle calculation singularities and image border text clipping on angled street signboards and smartphone-captured tilted documents. When processing documents or warning signs tilted at angles exceeding $\pm 10^\circ$, the automated deskewing algorithm cropped off words located near the left, right, top, and bottom canvas edges during rotation. For example, a heading like `CONFIDENTIAL REPORT` was clipped to `NFIDENTIAL REPO` because the rotated image corners extended beyond the fixed canvas boundaries. Additionally, on documents with prominent horizontal divider lines, the skew estimator produced an inverted $90^\circ$ angle, rotating the document sideways.

## 2. Steps to Reproduce
1. In the Streamlit dashboard, select the 1-Click Benchmark Demo for "Tilted Signboard" (`demo_type="tilted_signboard"`) or upload a photo tilted at 15 degrees.
2. Observe the deskewed and annotated output image.
3. Notice that letters at the far left and right edges are sliced off and missing from the extracted text string.
4. On documents containing horizontal dividers or barcode lines, observe the image erroneously rotating 90 degrees sideways.

## 3. Expected vs. Actual Behavior
- **Expected:** Tilted documents and signboards must be deskewed to zero degrees without clipping a single character near the margins, preserving full textual completeness.
- **Actual:** Fixed-size canvas rotation caused edge characters to be permanently chopped off, and divider lines triggered false 90-degree sideways rotations.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis
1. `rotate_image()` used basic `cv2.getRotationMatrix2D(center, angle, 1.0)` with a fixed output size `(w, h)`. When an image of width $W$ and height $H$ is rotated by angle $\theta$, its required bounding envelope expands to $W_{\text{new}} = H|\sin\theta| + W|\cos\theta|$ and $H_{\text{new}} = H|\cos\theta| + W|\sin\theta|$. Without canvas expansion, all pixels outside the original rectangle are clipped.
2. `estimate_skew_angle()` calculated contour bounding boxes without aspect ratio constraints. Horizontal divider lines with high width-to-height ratios ($w \gg h$) produced perpendicular angle readings, causing the rotation engine to twist documents sideways.

## 5. Developer Resolution (Applied by Software Developer Agent)
- Upgraded `src/utils.py` with **Smart Bounding Canvas Expansion & Aspect-Ratio Constraints**:
  - Implemented dynamic bounding box expansion in `rotate_image()`:
    $$\text{new\_w} = \operatorname{int}(H \cdot |\sin\theta| + W \cdot |\cos\theta|), \quad \text{new\_h} = \operatorname{int}(H \cdot |\cos\theta| + W \cdot |\sin\theta|)$$
  - Adjusted the rotation center coordinates and padded newly exposed border areas with pure white background pixels (`borderValue=(255, 255, 255)`).
  - Enforced aspect-ratio orientation correction ($w < h \implies \text{angle} = \text{angle} + 90$) and applied median angle filtering across contours in `estimate_skew_angle()` to eliminate false perpendicular divider line readings.
